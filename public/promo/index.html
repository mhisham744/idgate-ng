Under the hood

A three-dimensional identity model

IDGate never treats an account as a flat login. Three dimensions compose into every identity you carry.

DIMENSION 01

Natural person

A KYC'd human — your personal account. Ungoverned by profiles: you own your own data, contacts and communication. This is who signs in on Mobile.

DIMENSION 02

Legal entity

An organization: its legal data, four communication structures, positions and authorization profiles. Built and governed on Web.

DIMENSION 03

Position (virtual)

A seat inside an entity, linked to a person for a validity period. Switch to it and you act with its permissions — and carry its QR badge.
