The split

Same platform. Two kinds of people.

IDGate's account model draws a natural line. The people who carry and use identities live on mobile. The people who build and govern organizations work on the web.

Mobile · the end-user

Be your identities

The citizen & the position-holder

A person signs in once and switches between their personal self and any organization seats they hold — communicating, responding, and proving who they are, all from a phone.

  • Social feed, messages & official notifications
  • QR identity badge + Add to Apple Wallet
  • Accept/reject, respond, rate & vote
  • Switch between personal & position identities
Web · the maintainer

Run your organization

The organization-maintainer

An entity owner builds the organization behind the identities: communication structures, positions, authorization profiles, and the links that make a person a position-holder.

  • Register & activate legal entities
  • Build 4 communication structures (org charts)
  • Define positions, profiles & permissions
  • Link positions to people, delegate & govern

Under the hood

A three-dimensional identity model

IDGate never treats an account as a flat login. Three dimensions compose into every identity you carry.

DIMENSION 01

Natural person

A KYC'd human — your personal account. Ungoverned by profiles: you own your own data, contacts and communication. This is who signs in.

DIMENSION 02

Legal entity

An organization: its legal data, four communication structures, positions and authorization profiles. Built and governed on the web.

DIMENSION 03

Position (virtual)

A seat inside an entity, linked to a person for a validity period. When you switch to it, you act with its permissions — and carry its QR badge.
