# IDGate — Business Overview

**IDGate is a single, secure app where a person manages their verified identity and handles all
their official communication in one place.** Think of it as a national "identity gateway": you prove
who you are once, then use that trusted identity to represent yourself, your company, or any role
you hold — all from one bilingual (Arabic/English) app that works on phone and desktop.

**Live demo:** https://mhisham744.github.io/idgate-ng/

---

## The problem it solves

Official communication today is scattered across email, paper, phone calls, and disconnected
portals. Nothing is verified, nothing is tracked, and there's no single place to see "what needs my
attention." IDGate brings identity, messaging, and actionable notifications together — verified and
organized.

## What a user can do

- **Prove their identity once** through a guided verification process, and carry a trusted
  "verified" status wherever they act.
- **Act as more than one identity** — a personal account plus separate identities for the companies,
  entities, or roles they represent, switching between them instantly.
- **Send and receive official messages** with read tracking and file attachments.
- **Manage organizations** — company profiles, org charts, teams/groups, a searchable directory,
  and job vacancies.

## The centerpiece: smart notifications

Notifications aren't just alerts — they're a **two-way, trackable communication and task system**:

- A single notice can go to **many recipients at once**, and **each recipient has their own private
  conversation** with the sender. What one recipient says or decides is never visible to the others.
- Every recipient can **respond with a clear status** — Accept, Reject, ask for Clarification, or
  mark it Closed — so the sender always knows exactly where each person stands.
- If a recipient needs more information, they can send a **short clarification**, and the sender can
  **reply**, keeping the whole back-and-forth in one place.
- The sender stays in control: they can **edit** the original notice or **freeze** a conversation to
  stop further replies.
- **Attachments** can be added to any notice.

## "What needs my attention" at a glance

The home screen has a simple activity bar that pulls together everything across **all** of a
person's identities:

| Button | Answers the question |
|---|---|
| **Message** | Which messages haven't I read? |
| **Notification** | What have I been sent that I haven't responded to yet? |
| **Pending** | Which of the things *I* sent are still open on the other side? |
| **Duties** | What did I agree to that I still need to finish? |
| **Calendar** | What has an upcoming due date? |
| **Status** | Am I available, busy, or away? |

Nothing gets lost, and both sides always know the current state.
